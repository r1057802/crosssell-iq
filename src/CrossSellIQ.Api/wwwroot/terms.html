What this website is

CrossSellIQ is a student project by Nicolas Diaz Valencia for the Cloud & Ops and Artificial Intelligence courses at Thomas More. It shows which product categories an existing customer in the H&M dataset has not bought yet. It is a demo for education and is not a service of H&M, Kaggle, or Thomas More.

What the results mean

The current version recommends the most popular categories a customer has not bought yet. The score shows how popular a category is across all customers in the dataset. It is not a prediction or a probability that a customer will buy something, and it is not advice.

Data

The data comes from the Kaggle competition "H&M Personalized Fashion Recommendations" and is used under the rules of that competition. Do not copy or collect the data from this website to use it elsewhere.
